## Cach dat ve
Khach hang dang nhap vao ung dung, chon diem di, diem den va ngay khoi hanh tren man hinh Trang Chu. Sau do chon chuyen xe phu hop, chon ghe, kiem tra thong tin tai man hinh thanh toan va bam tiep tuc de thanh toan qua VNPAY. Neu thanh toan thanh cong, ve se duoc xac nhan va hien thi trong muc Ve cua toi.

## Thanh toan VNPAY
He thong hien ho tro thanh toan qua VNPAY sandbox. Khi thanh toan, khach hang se duoc chuyen sang cong thanh toan VNPAY de nhap thong tin the/test case. Sau khi giao dich thanh cong, VNPAY chuyen ve trang xac nhan cua backend va backend cap nhat booking sang trang thai CONFIRMED.

## Xem ve da dat
Khach hang co the xem ve trong tab Ve cua toi o thanh dieu huong ben duoi ung dung. Man hinh nay lay danh sach ve theo tai khoan dang dang nhap tu backend, bao gom ma ve, tuyen duong, gio khoi hanh, gio den du kien, ghe, bien so xe, tong tien va trang thai booking.

## Khong thay ve sau khi thanh toan
Neu khach hang da thanh toan thanh cong nhung chua thay ve, hay thu bam lai tab Ve cua toi hoac mo lai ung dung de tai lai du lieu tu backend. Neu backend chua nhan IPN/return tu VNPAY, trang thai co the van dang PENDING trong thoi gian ngan. Neu van khong thay ve, hay lien he ho tro voi ma booking hoac email tai khoan.

## Email xac nhan
Sau khi booking duoc tao, he thong co the gui email thong bao giu cho. Sau khi thanh toan thanh cong, he thong gui email xac nhan ve dien tu toi email tai khoan da dang ky. Neu khong thay email, khach hang nen kiem tra muc spam, promotion hoac xac nhan email dang ky co dung hay khong.

## Huy ve va hoan tien
O phien ban hien tai, ung dung chua co chuc nang huy ve tu dong tren giao dien Android. Booking chua thanh toan co the bi he thong huy neu qua han thanh toan. Voi ve da thanh toan, khach hang can lien he bo phan ho tro de duoc kiem tra va xu ly theo chinh sach.

## Lien he ho tro
Neu can ho tro them, khach hang co the lien he Bus Go Tickets qua email travelk.busticketbooking@gmail.com. Khi lien he, nen cung cap email tai khoan, ma booking, tuyen duong va thoi gian giao dich de he thong kiem tra nhanh hon.

## Loi ket noi backend
Neu ung dung bao khong ket noi duoc backend, hay dam bao backend dang chay, dien thoai/emulator truy cap dung dia chi IP local va cung mang voi may tinh chay backend. Voi Android emulator co the dung 10.0.2.2, voi dien thoai that nen dung IP LAN cua may tinh.
